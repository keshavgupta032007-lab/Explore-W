About Explore-W

Our mission

Explore-W brings flight, hotel, train, bus, cab and holiday-package search into one simple place, so you compare options instead of juggling tabs. This build shows a modern travel-booking experience using plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with your bookings kept safely in your browser.

What we offer

Flights, hotels, trains, buses, cabs and holiday packages, with clear comparisons, transparent pricing and a booking flow that takes a minute, not ten.

How it works

Search a service from the homepage, compare results side by side, and confirm your trip. Everything you book appears in My Trips, ready to review or cancel any time.
